## FAQ

**Q1: Is this actually free? What's the catch?**
Yes, completely free to use. No credit card. No login required. You run your audit, see your results instantly, and only then do we ask for your email if you want to save the report. The tool is free because it's built by Credex — if your audit shows significant savings, Credex can help you capture even more through discounted AI credits. That's the business model. No hidden fees, no bait and switch.

**Q2: Do you store my data? Is my spend information safe?**
Your audit inputs — tools, plans, spend amounts — are stored only to generate your result and your shareable link. We strip all identifying information (name, email, company) from the public shareable version. Your email is never sold or shared. We don't ask for API keys, passwords, or any account access. You type in numbers. We do the math.

**Q3: How accurate is the audit? Where does your pricing data come from?**
Every price in the audit engine traces back to an official vendor pricing page — Cursor, Anthropic, OpenAI, GitHub, Google. We verify and update pricing weekly. The recommendations are based on real plan structures and real usage patterns, not guesses. A finance person should be able to read our reasoning and agree with it. If you spot an error, email us and we'll fix it within 24 hours.

**Q4: I'm an indie dev with a small spend. Is this tool even relevant for me?**
Yes — especially for you. Indie devs are the most likely to overpay because they set up tools fast, never revisit them, and have no one reviewing the bills. Even saving $50–$100/month matters when you're funding your own side project. And if you're using API keys to build a product, the savings compound fast as your usage grows. Small spend now doesn't mean small waste.

**Q5: What is Credex and why did they build this?**
Credex is a marketplace for discounted AI and cloud credits — sourced from companies that overforecast or pivoted and ended up with unused credits. The discount is real: typically 40–45% off retail. SpendSmart is a free tool Credex built because most developers and startups don't realise they're overspending in the first place. The audit surfaces the problem. For teams with significant savings opportunities, Credex is one solution. But the audit is honest — if you're already spending well, we'll tell you that too.
